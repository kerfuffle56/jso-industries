Your advocate from procurement to closeout on fiber builds, data centers, and AI compute campuses. MARKETS WE'RE WATCHING

Every one of these lands on the ground.

New infrastructure categories are announced every quarter. Most are years from breaking ground. But all of them will need land, utility power, fiber routes, and someone competent standing on the site. We track them now so our clients are positioned early.

SATELLITE GROUND SEGMENT

Direct to cell constellations don't eliminate ground infrastructure. They relocate it. Every satellite network terminates at gateway earth stations. Sited, permitted, fiber fed, power served facilities that have to be found, negotiated, and built. Coverage moves to orbit. Capacity, and construction, stays on the ground.

QUANTUM COMPUTING FACILITIES

Quantum machines are among the most demanding tenants ever proposed. Cryogenic cooling plants, vibration isolated structures, exotic power quality, specialized fiber interconnection. When these facilities move from lab to campus, they will be construction projects first and physics second.

GRID & POWER INFRASTRUCTURE

Interconnection is the bottleneck of the entire digital buildout. Substations, transmission upgrades, behind the meter generation, microgrids, and small modular reactors are becoming part of the data center scope itself. Which makes power literacy part of construction oversight, not adjacent to it.

THE NEXT NETWORK

6G densification, edge inference sites, autonomous systems corridors. Whatever wins the next cycle will follow the same law as the last one. It pays rent to land, power, and fiber. We don't bet on which technology wins. We work the layer they all depend on.

Watching a market is free. Being ready when it breaks ground is the business.
