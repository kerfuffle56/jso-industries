SERVICES

What we do.

01 / OWNER SIDE

Owner's Representation

Your advocate from procurement to closeout. We draft and evaluate bids, manage contractors, review pay applications against work in place, track schedule and budget, and report to you in plain language. You stay in control without living on the job site.

02 / LEASES & SITES

Lease & Site Advisory

Carrier leases, rooftop and tower agreements, fiber routes, easements, and rights of way. We know what the installation actually involves, so you negotiate from the construction reality, not the carrier's template.

03 / DUE DILIGENCE

Technical Due Diligence

Buying, funding, or underwriting infrastructure? We assess build quality, cost to complete, contractor performance, and schedule risk before you commit capital. Then monitor the asset after you do.
